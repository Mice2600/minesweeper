Privacy Policy — Minesweeper Co-op

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Minesweeper Co-op ("the app", "we") is a cooperative multiplayer Minesweeper game. This policy explains what data the app handles and why. We aim to collect as little as possible.

No accounts

The app has no sign-up, login, or user account. You are identified within a game only by a display name and avatar you choose, which are not tied to any real-world identity.

Data stored on your device

The following is saved locally on your device and is not sent to us:

  • Your chosen display name and avatar image.
  • Your preferred difficulty.
  • In-game coins and which cosmetic board skins you own or have equipped.

This data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Multiplayer data

When you host or join a game, the app exchanges gameplay messages with the other players in your room. Depending on the mode:

  • Local Wi-Fi (LAN): your device connects directly to the other players on the same network. Nothing passes through our servers.
  • Online (room code): messages are relayed through a Cloudflare Worker that routes traffic by room code. The relay is protocol-blind — it forwards game messages between players verbatim and does not store game content or sell any data.

Messages exchanged with other players in your room include: your display name and avatar, board actions (cell reveals and flags), cursor position, emoji reactions, and chat messages you send. Do not share personal information in chat or your display name, as other players in the room can see it.

Network information

To connect players, the app uses standard network connectivity (and, on local Wi-Fi, multicast discovery to find nearby games). As with any internet service, IP addresses are visible to the relay and to other hosts purely to route the connection; they are not stored by us for tracking purposes.

Advertising and analytics

The Android version shows ads via Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process your device's advertising identifier and related device information. For users in the EEA/UK, a consent prompt is shown before personalized ads are used. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings. See Google's Privacy & Terms.

The app also uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand aggregate usage (e.g. matches played, screens viewed). This is used to improve the game and is not used to identify you personally.

Ads and these advertising identifiers are not used on the web or Windows versions, which do not include the ads SDK.

In-app purchases

The Android version offers optional in-app purchases: coin packs (coins buy cosmetic board skins only and never affect gameplay) and a tip jar. All payments are processed by Google Play Billing — we never see or store your card details, billing address, or any other payment information. Google's handling of that data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. Purchased coins are stored on your device.

Content from other players, and your safety tools

Multiplayer means you can see content other people create: their display name, profile picture, chat messages, and emoji reactions. We do not review this content before you see it. The app includes the following controls:

  • Automatic filtering. Display names and chat messages are screened for offensive language by the device hosting the game before anyone in the room sees them. No filter is perfect.
  • Block. Tap any player — in the lobby, the in-game player bar, or on one of their chat messages — and choose Block. Their messages, reactions, cursor, and profile picture are hidden from you. Blocks are stored on your device and persist between games.
  • Report. The same menu lets you report a player with a reason. The report is sent to the host of that game, who can remove them. A copy — including the reported player's recent messages — is saved on your device under About → Safety, where you can email it to us at the address below if the host takes no action.
  • Remove from game. If you are hosting, you can disconnect any player; they cannot rejoin that room.

Blocks and reports never leave your device except for the report sent to the game's host, and any report you choose to email us. We keep emailed reports only as long as needed to look into them.

Data retention

We operate no user database. Multiplayer messages pass through the relay in memory to reach the other players in your room and are not written to disk or retained after the room closes. Everything else — your name, avatar, coins, skins, blocked players, and report history — is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the app.

Children

The app is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can clear all locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the app. You control your display name, avatar, whether you join online or local-only games, and which players you block. You can reset or limit your advertising ID in your device's system settings, and manage or clear your blocked-player and report lists under About → Safety in the app.
